Design question

How might a service desk preserve context and accountability from intake to resolution?

01

Decision context

Ticket cards surface the minimum information needed for triage without opening every request.

02

Priority ≠ status

Separate urgency from workflow state so teams can scan what matters and what is moving.

03

Reversible workflows

Search, filters, ticket creation, and state feedback are designed for operational recovery.
